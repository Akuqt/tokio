-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Rust crates
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

The installer is written in Rust and statically links its crates,
including wry and tao (the window and webview, by the Tauri Programme
within The Commons Conservancy), ureq, serde and serde_json. Almost all of
them are licensed under MIT and/or Apache-2.0; the complete list, with
each crate's license text and repository, is in src/installer/licenses/
rust-crates.txt (embedded as rust-crates.txt).

On macOS and Linux, wry depends on option-ext 0.2.0, licensed under the
Mozilla Public License 2.0 (https://mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/, also in
LICENSES/MPL-2.0.txt). It is used unmodified. Its source code is
available at https://crates.io/crates/option-ext and
https://github.com/soc/option-ext.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------
System web views
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

The installer shows its user interface with the web view that comes with
the operating system: Microsoft Edge WebView2 on Windows, WebKit
(WKWebView) on macOS and WebKitGTK on Linux. They are not included in the
installer. On Linux, WebKitGTK and GTK are licensed under the GNU LGPL
and are loaded from the system as shared libraries.
